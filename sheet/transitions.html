Clawd Transitions

Every switch the plugin makes between animations, played with its real frames on its one-second tick. Every loop starts and ends on the same standing pose. A switch plays the current animation's exit (-out, dashed, with its length): the loop plays on until Clawd is back to standing, held items ease away and the props leave. Then the next animation makes its entrance. Writing and stretch share the laptop and switch straight across (~).
